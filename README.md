# Build-Tableau-Next-Consulting-Skills-with-Analytics-Con-202-Practice-Questions
A Tableau Next implementation involves choices that extend beyond a dashboard. Someone has to decide how data should be modeled, which users can access the resulting assets, and where those assets belong in everyday work. Analytics-Con-202 brings those decisions together through the Salesforce Certified Tableau Next Consultant certification.

When turning the exam outline into a study plan, CertQueen [Analytics-Con-202 exam questions](https://www.certqueen.com/Analytics-Con-202.html) can fit naturally between reading and practical review. Use each attempt to identify a decision you cannot yet explain, then return to the relevant Salesforce material before trying another scenario.

## Know the assessment you are preparing for

The certification targets practitioners who implement and maintain scalable analytics solutions within Salesforce. Data analysts, business intelligence consultants, and Salesforce architects are among the intended candidates.

The verified exam details are:

- 60 multiple-choice questions, with up to five additional unscored questions.
- 105 minutes to complete the assessment.
- A passing score of 65%.
- English language delivery.
- Registration of USD 200 or JPY 30,000, plus applicable taxes.
- Retakes of USD 100 or JPY 15,000, plus applicable taxes.
- Online or testing-center proctoring, with no reference materials permitted.
- No mandatory certification prerequisite.

The guide aligns the questions to the July 262.11 release. Check the current guide before booking, particularly if your study material describes a different release.

## Follow the complete implementation scope

Data Setup accounts for 20%. It covers semantic model components and design, basic Data 360 capabilities, and choosing an architecture or processing approach for a scenario. Practice explaining how a proposed model answers a business question before discussing its visual presentation.

Agentic Experiences carries 25%, the largest share. Study generative AI support for creating Tableau Next components, agentic readiness, Analytics Agent exploration, and proactive alerts. Distinguish preparing an experience for use from simply activating a feature.

Embedding, Cross-Cloud, & Interoperability contributes 20%. Review integration with Salesforce analytics platforms, surfacing assets in user workflows, Tableau Next Apps for Salesforce, Marketplace offerings, and developer tools and APIs.

Basic Setup & Admin represents 10%. Its objectives concern feature activation, appropriate user access, and enabling Agentic Analytics. Visualizations & Dashboards adds 15%, with attention to usable designs and configuring actions.

Managing Workspaces & Orgs completes the outline at 10%. It includes Personal Org deployment and management, sharing assets, and deploying Tableau Next assets. Keep these operational responsibilities in your revision plan even if your daily role focuses on analysis.

## Connect the domains through one example

As a study exercise, imagine a service manager asking for a shared view of team performance. Write down the question the manager wants answered and the data assumptions behind it.

Next, describe the semantic model, the intended audience, and the workflow where the analysis should appear. Consider whether an Analytics Agent experience would help users explore the same information. Finish by explaining how you would review sharing and deployment.

This is an illustrative learning exercise, not an additional official objective. Its purpose is to expose weak connections between topics that may seem straightforward when studied separately.

## Build a preparation routine around explanations

Start with the official Salesforce exam guide and its preparation trail. SDC013 and TNX001 are recommended courses. Practical experience with Salesforce Setup, Data 360, and Tableau Next should accompany that reading.

After each study session, describe one design choice without consulting your notes. If you can name a feature but cannot explain why it fits the scenario, revisit the underlying requirement. Keep uncertain release-specific details beside the source you need to check.

The recommended Platform Administrator, Data 360 Consultant, and Agentforce Specialist certifications can provide related background. They are recommendations, rather than prerequisites for this exam.

## Make practice questions reveal gaps

For each answer, identify the requirement that determines your choice. Record why an alternative would be less suitable, and review correct guesses alongside incorrect responses.

Group mistakes by objective rather than by question number. Several errors involving user access may point to one administrative misunderstanding. A different wording should not require you to relearn the same concept.

Return to an uncertain topic after a break and explain it again before checking the answer. This makes practice a test of understanding rather than a test of whether you remember the previous attempt.

## Understand what the credential supports

The certification validates consulting skills across data preparation, analytics experiences, and implementation responsibilities. Its scope gives practitioners a structured way to review the decisions involved in a maintainable Tableau Next solution.

Begin with the complete outline, deepen the areas where your experience is limited, and use practice to guide the next review session. After earning the credential, complete the required annual Trailhead maintenance badge to keep it current.
